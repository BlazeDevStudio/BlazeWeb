B Secure / Legal

Privacy policy.

Effective November 13, 2026. Applies to the B Secure Chrome extension, including version 11.5.0.

Overview

B Secure protects Chrome browsing sessions using a password you configure. Its password verification, settings and tab recovery operate locally. The extension does not require an account and does not send stored password hashes or tab URLs to BlazeDevStudio.

Data handled by the extension

  • Authentication information. When you set or enter a password, B Secure processes it on your device and stores a salted PBKDF2-SHA256 hash, salt and verification parameters. It does not retain the plaintext password in extension storage or transmit it to the developer.
  • Tab URLs. When locking tabs, B Secure saves URLs locally so it can return to them after unlocking. URLs can include sensitive search terms and other personal details. B Secure does not intentionally create a complete browsing-history log or read the contents of web pages.
  • Settings and security events. B Secure stores your failed-attempt limit, cooldown, relock configuration, attempt counters and session timestamps. It observes browser tab/window events to operate its lock and restoration functions.

Use and sharing

B Secure uses Chrome's local and session extension storage. It has no developer-operated login server, analytics service, or tracking scripts. It does not sell stored data or share it with advertisers. The data described above is used for the extension's password-lock and tab-restoration features.

Settings may display an optional PayPal donation QR image bundled with the extension. If you choose to visit PayPal, information you give PayPal is handled under PayPal's own privacy practices. B Secure itself does not process payment credentials.

Storage, retention, and deletion

Password verification records and settings remain in the Chrome profile until changed, cleared, or removed. Saved URL records are usually deleted once tabs are restored. Orphaned records may remain for recovery and can be removed during cleanup after 30 days. Temporary session information follows Chrome's extension storage lifecycle.

Uninstalling B Secure or clearing its storage removes local extension data but may also erase saved information needed to restore parked tabs. If you forget your password, BlazeDevStudio cannot recover it. Removing or disabling the extension may bypass its lock; deleting an entire Chrome profile is not always required.

Security limits

B Secure is an extension-level browser guard, not an operating-system authentication system. A person who can modify the Chrome profile or disable extensions may be able to bypass it. Chrome's own restricted pages are not fully controlled by extensions.
