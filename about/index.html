01 / Background

Built through practice.

My starting point was computer programming in senior high school, followed by undergraduate study in Mechanical Engineering Technology. Alongside that, I learned by repairing PCs and laptops, troubleshooting networks and working with electronic components.

That practical side is still central to how I work. I’m comfortable moving between Windows troubleshooting, small-business network deployments, microcontroller projects and software prototypes. I like understanding how a system behaves from the hardware up—not just making the interface look finished.

BlazeDevStudio is where I organize those builds. Blaze Systems covers software and hardware work, ManrealTech covers repairs and IT support, and Blaze Labs holds experiments and early ideas.
