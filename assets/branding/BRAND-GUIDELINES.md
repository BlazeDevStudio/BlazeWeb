# BlazeDevStudio Brand Identity v2.0 — Ruby Module
Date: 2026-10-29. Status: current website identity / candidate brand master.

## Brand mark
The robust B-shaped blue ribbon remains the parent identity. A small high-chroma ruby signal at its upper-right edge is the family identifier. The ruby module is deliberately detachable and can identify individual projects without redrawing the B.

The master 4096×4096 PNG, vector source, transparent variant, and eight ruby project symbols are archived in the [Drive brand package](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1XJHo4OYxkpsxgzDOfLJl1pumgx6gWSX4). The earlier thin-line favicon is superseded; historical versions remain available through Git history.

## Rules
- Main logo: bold silhouette, unbroken white insets, simple blue diagonal; no orbit around the entire B.
- Module: ruby-red center with open blue/white ring. Category indicated by a single simple white glyph (core, hardware, software, network, media, repair, labs or projects).
- Favicon: use simplified 64-unit vector `favicon.svg`; avoid miniature ring details.
- Background `#090F1D`, white `#F4F7FF`, blue-topaz `#72B2FF`, ruby `#E40035`.
- Preserve 1:1 proportions, eight-percent outer space, monochrome usability, high contrast and legibility at 16, 32, 128 px.
- Do not add extra type inside the mark, external glows or unrelated graphics.

## Provenance
Hand-redrawn SVG interpretation of owner-selected AI mock-up; not an exact pixel tracing. Before registering a trademark, perform independent clearance.
